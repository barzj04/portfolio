about

I'm a Computer Science student at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP) in my final year, with a minor in finance. Most of what I build sits where data meets real-world problems: machines, money, air quality, everyday life.

My final year project is an IoT predictive maintenance system for oil & gas equipment. It goes from an ESP32 vibration sensor all the way to an explainable ML model and a live dashboard. Outside class I join hackathons and data competitions, and I build small apps for the people around me.

I like projects that actually get used, even if the users are just my housemates.
